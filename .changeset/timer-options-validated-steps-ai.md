The remaining options that reach a timer are checked with `assertTimerMs`, with the same rule and `RangeError` as the hosts, before any side effect. The AI helper's per-call `timeoutMs` and `timeout.perCallMs` (checked when the helper is created) must be a number from 1 to 2147483647: `0` used to time the call out at once and a value above 2147483647 ms fired after 1 ms; `undefined` is still the only "no timeout". The step API's `heartbeat` (the `setInterval` that extends the lease) and `lease` (the longest wait of the stage's in-flight steps before a suspension, which fired at once above 2147483647 ms) must be from 1 to 2147483647 too, and `heartbeat` or `lease` of `NaN`, `Infinity` or a negative number now gets the same message instead of `heartbeat must be a positive finite number` or `Invalid duration`; the check runs before the step is claimed. `ctx.step.ai.map`'s `realtime.retryDelayMs` and `realtime.minDelayMs` accept `0` (off, the default) or 1 to 2147483647, as milliseconds or a duration string such as `"30s"`; `"25d"` used to fire after 1 ms, and the check runs before any model call. Values inside the ranges behave as before.
