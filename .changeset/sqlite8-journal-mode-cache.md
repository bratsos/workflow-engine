Prisma 8 SQLite: a write on the client's root connection is no longer lost after that connection leaves WAL. In the SQLite that `node:sqlite` ships on Node 22 and 24, a connection that leaves WAL through `PRAGMA journal_mode` keeps its page cache as of its last read, even though another connection, such as an engine transaction's, committed since, and an unqualified change does the same to every attached database. Its next write then dropped rows or left damaged indexes in the file. `fromPrisma8` now drops the root connection's page cache with `PRAGMA shrink_memory` after any root statement whose text contains `journal_mode` (whatever its spelling), whether the statement succeeded or threw, whether it is sent through the wrapper or through `db.query`, `db.orm` or `db.sql`. `shrink_memory` only releases the connection's own memory: in every schedule tested it neither waited, failed busy nor left a statement open on the root (SQLite documents no lock for it), and it cannot free the pages of a read still handing over rows (SQLite refuses the change then). A mode change made before the wrapper's takeover, from a plain Prisma 8 client or from another process, is not covered and can still lose rows another connection committed: make it before the engine starts.
