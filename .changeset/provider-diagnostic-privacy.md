Text capture now covers error text on AI call rows. Under `store: "none"` or a TTL, a row's `metadata.error` is stored as `AI call failed`, whichever path wrote it: the helper's own calls, a stock helper given the stage's `ctx.aiLogger`, batch results, `recordCall` and direct `ctx.aiLogger` writes. A TTL row never stores the message, because text expiry clears prompt, response and instructions only. The engine's own console lines print provider error text only when both `WORKFLOW_ENGINE_TRACE=1` and `WORKFLOW_ENGINE_TRACE_TEXT=1` are set; otherwise a failed call prints its error name and HTTP status. Provider warnings reach the SDK, step callbacks and `rawResult` unchanged, as before, and the SDK prints them as process warnings unless `globalThis.AI_SDK_LOG_WARNINGS = false`. A provider error message can contain text the provider echoed from the request. Values thrown to callers and the SDK, stage and step error columns, events, outbox payloads, workflow logs and console reads keep it, as before. Direct stage accounting writes through `ctx.aiLogger` follow the policy and attribution of the helper the `services.ai` factory returns, including a child helper and a wrapper that spreads the helper or wraps it in a `Proxy` or `Object.create`. A wrapper that hides the helper it delegates to gets the strictest text policy among the helpers the factory made and the run's attribution, so it should spread or proxy the helper to keep its labels. Frozen loggers and providers keep working.

**Rolling-safe** for schema compatibility. Enforce the privacy policy after all writers support it and address previously captured data separately.
